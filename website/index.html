I. The Farm

It began with a little rice.

Scott and Lindsay Meyer started Congaree & Penn as a tiny rice farm in 2014. It has grown into orchards, vines and fields, with honeybees, guinea hens and mayhaws, restaurants, oyster roasts and chef's tastings among the grapevines. The Eleven is how a few families get to stay.

“We've spent years getting to know this land: its seasons, its soil, the sound of the guineas at first light. The Eleven is our way of sharing it with a few families who want to live close to something real.” Scott & Lindsay Meyer · Founders

II. The Land

Not lots. Claims.

You don't buy a Claim so much as make one, then prove it up over time: a pasture walked, a porch raised, a barn named. Each is ten acres along the farm's western edge, with Cary State Forest to the north.

III. The Life

The best reason to live here is the life here.

Quiet luxury, as we mean it, is slowing down: being present, and enjoying the land and the people around you. Oyster fires by the pond, long tables beneath the grapevines, dogs under the oaks. Owners of The Eleven live it every season.

  • Farm-to-table restaurants
  • Fall at the Farm oyster roasts
  • Chef's tastings at the Grapevine
  • Trails into Cary State Forest
  • Room for horses & chickens*
  • Mayhaws, figs & olives

*Confirm livestock allowances for your Claim with John.

Questions, answered

Before you walk the land.

What is The Eleven at Congaree & Penn?

The Eleven is a community of eleven ten-acre farmsteads, called Claims, set within Congaree & Penn, a working farm on Old Kings Road in Jacksonville, Florida. Owners also have about 200 more acres of farm to wander, and Cary State Forest borders the community to the north.

How much does a Claim cost?

As of November 2026, ten-acre Claims are priced from $750,000 to $1,200,000. The price is for the Claim (land) only; the home is designed and built separately.

How many Claims are still available?

Nine of the eleven remain. Claim No. 4 has closed, and the first home is under construction on Claim No. 10. There will only ever be eleven.

Can I keep horses or livestock?

The Claims are agricultural and laid out for horses, chickens and other livestock. Allowances should be confirmed for each Claim with John Hillman.

How long does it take to build?

Custom homes typically take 18 to 24 months, depending on the design and the builder.

Who designs and builds the homes?

A curated Collective of architects, builders, landscape and interior designers, including Pearce Scott Architects, Clarke Design Group, Court Atkins Group, Riverside Custom Homes and DF Luxury Homes.

How do I purchase a Claim?

Select your Claim, sign the Purchase and Sale Agreement with a $25,000 earnest deposit, and close in 30 days or less.
